Got Questions?

Quick Answers

Walk-ins are welcome but we recommend booking online to guarantee your slot, especially at weekends and during school holidays. Booking takes less than 2 minutes!

FunVille is designed for children aged 0–12. We have a dedicated Toddler Town for under-4s with soft, safe equipment, and bigger zones for older adventurers. Under-2s are free!

Comfortable clothes and grip socks are required for all children. Grip socks are available to purchase at reception for £2.50 if you forget yours. Adults must remove shoes before entering play zones.

You're welcome to bring snacks and drinks for children. We ask that you eat in the café area, not in the play zones. Our café also serves freshly made food and snacks at great prices.

Simply pick a package above and click Reserve, or contact our party team directly at parties@funville.co. We recommend booking at least 4 weeks in advance for weekend dates. A 25% deposit secures your booking.

Yes! Free parking is available directly adjacent to FunVille with 80 spaces including 6 accessible bays. We're also a 5-minute walk from the town centre bus stops.
